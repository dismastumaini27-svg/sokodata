Kuhusu sisiAbout us

Taarifa za bei zilizo wazi kwa kila MtanzaniaOpen price information for every Tanzanian

SokoData Tanzania ni jukwaa huru linalokusanya, kupanga na kuchapisha bei za mazao, chakula na mifugo kutoka vyanzo rasmi.SokoData Tanzania is an independent platform that collects, organises and publishes crop, food and livestock prices from official sources.

Kwa nini tupoWhy we exist

Wizara ya Kilimo na Wizara ya Mifugo na Uvuvi hukusanya bei za masoko kila wiki. Lakini taarifa hizo huchapishwa kama PDF au picha, moja moja, na ni vigumu kulinganisha mikoa, kuona mwenendo wa miezi au kutumia takwimu hizo kwenye utafiti.The Ministry of Agriculture and the Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries collect market prices every week. But the reports come out as separate PDFs or images, so it is hard to compare regions, follow trends over months or use the figures in research.

SokoData inachukua kila taarifa, inasoma bei zake, inazihifadhi pamoja na chanzo, na kuzionyesha kwa njia rahisi: ramani, chati, majedwali, na data kwa ombi.SokoData takes each report, reads its prices, stores them with their source and shows them simply: a map, charts, tables, and data on request.

DhamiraMission

Kufanya bei za soko za Tanzania ziwe rahisi kupata, kulinganisha na kuamini, kwa kila mtu, bila malipo.To make Tanzanian market prices easy for anyone to find, compare and trust, free of charge.

DiraVision

Tanzania ambapo kila mkulima, mfanyabiashara na mtunga sera anafanya maamuzi kwa kutumia taarifa sahihi za bei, na ambapo masoko ya kilimo ni ya haki na wazi.A Tanzania where every farmer, trader and policy maker decides with accurate price information, and where agricultural markets are fair and transparent.

Maadili yetuOur values

UwaziOpenness

Msimbo uko wazi kwa yeyote kuboresha, na data inatolewa kwa ombi.The code is open for anyone to improve, and data is provided on request.

Uaminifu kuhusu mapungufuHonest about gaps
