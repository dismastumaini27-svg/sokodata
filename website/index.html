DhamiraMission

Kufanya bei za soko za Tanzania ziwe rahisi kupata, kulinganisha na kuamini, kwa kila mtu.To make Tanzanian market prices easy for anyone to find, compare and trust.

DiraVision

Tanzania ambapo kila mkulima na mfanyabiashara anafanya maamuzi kwa kutumia taarifa sahihi za bei.A Tanzania where every farmer and trader makes decisions with accurate price information.
