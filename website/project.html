ChangamotoThe problem

Mkulima anayeuza mahindi Rukwa mara nyingi hajui bei ya Dar es Salaam au Arusha wiki hii. Mfanyabiashara anajua. Pengo hilo la taarifa linamfanya mkulima kuuza kwa bei ya chini.A farmer selling maize in Rukwa often does not know this week's price in Dar es Salaam or Arusha. The trader does. That information gap makes farmers sell for less.

Takwimu zipo: wizara huzikusanya kila wiki. Lakini zimetawanyika kwenye mamia ya PDF, hazina historia inayounganika, na haziwezi kupakuliwa kama data.The figures exist: the ministries collect them every week. But they are scattered across hundreds of PDFs, with no joined-up history, and cannot be downloaded as data.

Jinsi inavyofanya kaziHow it works

  1. Kukusanya.Collect. Kila asubuhi programu inaangalia tovuti za Wizara ya Kilimo na Wizara ya Mifugo na Uvuvi kwa taarifa mpya, pamoja na magazeti kama The Citizen, Daily News na The Guardian.Every morning a program checks the Ministry of Agriculture and Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries websites for new reports, and newspapers such as The Citizen, Daily News and The Guardian.
  2. Kusoma.Read. Bei za kila mkoa na zao zinasomwa kutoka kwenye PDF na kuhifadhiwa pamoja na kiungo cha taarifa ya asili.Each region's and crop's price is read from the PDF and stored with a link to the original report.
  3. Kukagua.Check. Bei iliyobadilika zaidi ya 30% kwa wiki moja inawekwa alama ya ukaguzi; ripoti ya kila siku inaandikwa.Any price that moved more than 30% in a week is flagged for checking, and a daily report is written.
  4. Kuchapisha.Publish. Tovuti hii inasasishwa: ramani, chati, majedwali na faili za kupakua.This website is updated: map, charts, tables and download files.
