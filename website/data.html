Omba dataRequest data

Unahitaji data ambayo haipo hapa, kipindi kirefu zaidi, masoko maalum, au data kwa ajili ya utafiti au mradi? Tuambie unachohitaji, nasi tutakujibu kwa barua pepe.Need data that is not here: a longer period, specific markets, or data for research or a project? Tell us what you need and we will reply by email.

  • Watafiti na wanafunzi: data ya historia kwa ajili ya tasnifu na makalaResearchers and students: historical data for theses and papers
  • Mashirika na benki: bei za masoko maalum kwa kipindi maalumOrganisations and banks: prices for chosen markets and periods
  • Waandishi wa habari: takwimu za haraka zenye chanzoJournalists: quick figures with their source

Tunatumia barua pepe yako kukujibu tu; hatuitoi kwa mtu mwingine.We use your email only to reply to you; we do not share it.
